Getting certified is easy

1Join USA SwimmingRegister as an official. Includes a background check, concussion training, and Athlete Protection Training.
2Learn onlineTake Stroke & Turn training and Foundations of Officiating at your own pace.
3Shadow at a meetWork alongside experienced officials and learn by doing.
